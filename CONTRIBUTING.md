# Contributing to Microclaw

Microclaw welcomes contributions under the repository's BSD 3-Clause license.
Contributors retain copyright in their work; the collective copyright notice
"Microclaw contributors" in `LICENSE` does not transfer ownership to a project
maintainer or imply that one person owns every contribution.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

All commits contributed to this project must include a `Signed-off-by` trailer
certifying the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/):

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Add it with `git commit --signoff`. By signing off, you certify that you have
the right to submit the contribution under the project's BSD 3-Clause license
and agree that the contribution and sign-off are public records. The DCO is a
statement about the provenance of a contribution; it is not a copyright
assignment.

Do not contribute third-party code, data, papers, images, or other material
unless its license permits redistribution here and its required attribution
and license terms are recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
